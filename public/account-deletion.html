Delete a SmokeStack account

Your SmokeStack data belongs to you. Account deletion permanently removes the Firebase account, UID-scoped Firestore records, account overlays, and identifiable Community Smoker Database submissions associated with that account.

  1. Open SmokeStack and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings → Account.
  3. Select Delete account and cloud data.
  4. Enter the confirmation phrase shown by the app.

Local browser data is cleared on the device that completes deletion. Separately created Google Drive backups remain in your Google Drive and can be deleted there by you.
