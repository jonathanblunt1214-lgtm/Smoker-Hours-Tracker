Your data always belongs to you

SmokeStack receives no ownership interest in your cook logs, equipment records, photos, recipes, notes, preferences, backups, community submissions, or other account content. No upload, synchronization, backup, analysis, or contribution transfers title to SmokeStack.

Service and acceptance

SmokeStack provides cook logging, equipment and fuel records, planning, analytics, maintenance tools, optional Firebase account synchronization, optional Google Drive backup, optional CharGPT assistance, and provenance-backed community smoker information as those features are actually available. Accepting Revision 5 does not grant a device, Google, sharing, or AI permission. Material changes require a new revision and renewed acceptance before the changed practice begins.

Data collected and why

  • Firebase account identifiers support authentication and UID-scoped account access.
  • Cook logs, smoker and fuel records, settings, notes, and preferences provide the user-requested tracking, planning, analytics, synchronization, and personalization features.
  • Prompts and the cook, smoker, photo, or preference context a user chooses to send may be processed through Google Cloud and Google AI to answer a CharGPT request.
  • Submission-specific community smoker information is stored for provenance review and possible publication only after explicit consent.
  • Minimum operational and security events may be processed to protect accounts and diagnose the service; they do not become a hidden advertising profile.

Ownership and limited permission

The user grants only the limited, non-exclusive technical permission required to store, process, synchronize, display, export, and delete that user's data on the user's instructions and to provide selected features. The permission does not allow unrelated use and ends when the data or account is deleted, subject only to a specifically disclosed lawful retention requirement. SmokeStack does not sell user data or use private account data for third-party advertising.

Storage, retention, and service providers

Firebase Authentication establishes identity. Signed-in UID-scoped Firestore data is authoritative; signed-out data remains local to that browser. Firebase and Google Cloud process data for authentication, storage, security, server features, and selected CharGPT requests. Account data is retained until the user deletes the relevant records or account, subject to a specifically disclosed lawful retention requirement. Local data remains until the user clears it, deletes the account on that device, or browser storage is removed.

Google Drive

Google Drive is a separate optional backup controlled by the user's Google authorization. Disconnecting SmokeStack or deleting a SmokeStack account does not delete an existing Drive backup; the user owns and controls that file and can delete it in Drive.

Community Smoker Database

Each contribution requires an authenticated account and explicit consent for that submission. It starts as user-entered information pending review, not a manufacturer fact. The user retains ownership and grants only the limited permission to review, label, publish, and display the submission until withdrawal or associated account deletion. Broader federated cook-data sharing remains unavailable without a future granular consent workflow and separate acceptance.

Export, correction, and deletion

Users can correct records in the app and download JSON from Settings. Authenticated deletion removes the Firebase account, UID-scoped Firestore records and subcollections, account overlays, role records, and identifiable community submissions. It clears SmokeStack local data on the deleting device. Deletion failure remains visible and is never reported as success.
