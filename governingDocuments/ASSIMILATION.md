**Repository:** `jonathanblunt1214-lgtm/Smoker-Hours-Tracker`

**Purpose:** One-time assimilation of injected `governingDocuments/`

## Injection boundary

This instruction exists exclusively to govern assimilation of an authorized `governingDocuments/` injection.

Injection authority:
- does not widen repository authority;
- does not authorize direct writes to `main`;
- does not authorize bypassing native branch, review, CI, OWNER, security, or constitutional controls;
- applies only to authorized injected material;
- may not be reused as standing repository authority.

The receiving project must follow its native repository rules when creating the task branch, validating the change, opening any required pull request, and obtaining required OWNER approval.

## Native-level assimilation

Injected `governingDocuments/` MUST be assimilated at the receiving project's native governance level.

They must not remain an isolated sidecar, temporary overlay, agent-specific rule set, or lower-priority advisory copy.

Assimilation means the receiving project incorporates the injected governance into its normal AI-governance interpretation, handoff state, conflict handling, and enforcement mechanisms while preserving any pre-existing higher-priority native authority.

For Smoke Stack, the SmokeStack Engineering Constitution and its governing amendments remain controlling wherever they have higher native authority.

A conflict between injected governance and controlling native governance MUST NOT be silently resolved by overriding either side. Preserve the conflict and route it through the project's conflict-resolution process.

## Cooperative-agent assimilation

During assimilation, `AI-HANDOFF` and `AI-CONFLICTS` MUST establish that all authorized AI agents working on Smoke Stack work together from shared project state going forward.

Agents must consume relevant shared handoff state, preserve valid concurrent work, reconcile compatible changes, record genuine conflicts, never force-push or silently overwrite another agent's valid work, and defer genuinely unresolved governance conflicts to the OWNER or other native deciding authority.

No agent gains authority to impersonate another agent or expand its own permissions through this cooperation requirement.

4. verify `AI-CONFLICTS` represents cooperative multi-agent conflict handling;
5. verify no unrelated files were changed through injection authority;
6. verify `main` was not directly mutated;
7. record completion and sever temporary injection authority.

**Injection principle:** authorize narrowly, assimilate natively, preserve collaboration, verify, then sever.
